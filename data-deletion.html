Account & Data Deletion

Official User Data Governance • United Crypto Alliance (UCA)

In strict compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and global privacy frameworks (GDPR & CCPA), users of the United Crypto Alliance application (com.unitedcryptoalliance.app) have the full right to permanently delete their account and erase all associated personal data from our systems.

⚠️ Strict Security Notice: Send From Registered Email Only
To prevent unauthorized account deletion, our security desk strictly verifies that the deletion email is sent directly from your registered Google email address. Requests received from other addresses are automatically discarded.

Support destination: support@unitedcryptoalliance.com

What Happens When Your Data is Deleted?

  • Permanently Erased: Google Account UID, registered email, profile name, 24-hour mining timestamps, active session status, historical claimed and pending UCAT balances, and 6-digit invite code.
  • Timeframe: Verification and permanent database deletion occurs within 30 calendar days.
  • Notice: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Mined coin balances cannot be restored once purged.
